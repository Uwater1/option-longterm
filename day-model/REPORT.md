## Methodology Overview
1. **Lockbox Split**: From 2024-03-01 to last day (OOS holdout).
2. **Selection Validation Split**: Six non-contiguous 3-month blocks carved out from the working set, partitioned into 4 inner (tuning) + 2 outer (held-out) blocks, with a 10-day embargo.
3. **BH-FDR Screening**: Robust marginal Spearman correlation at FDR = 0.15 on selection train.
4. **Hierarchical Clustering**: Groups collinear features (|r| >= 0.75).
5. **Cluster Stability Selection + VIF Pruning**: B=100 subsamples, pi=0.60 cluster threshold, then VIF <= 10 pruning.
6. **Weighted Fitting**: Sample weights w(y) = |y|^k.
7. **Side-Aware Optuna Objective**:
   - `single` (legacy): Tail IC two-sided (top10% U bot10%). Weights V1..V4 = [0.40, 0.40, 0.15, 0.05].
   - `long`: Tail IC top-only (`pred >= P85(pred)`). Weights [0.35, 0.50, 0.15, 0.00] (V4 dropped).
   - `short`: Tail IC bot-only (`pred <= P15(pred)`). Weights [0.35, 0.50, 0.15, 0.00] (V4 dropped).
   - CV fold kill-switches (M1..M6: hit rate, monotonicity, spread, ESS, Gini) stay **two-sided** for all sides.
8. **Deflated Objective + Plateau Selection**: Running Lopez de Prado deflation; final trial chosen via plateau search (r=0.25).
9. **15-Panel Diagnostics Figure per Tag**: One combined PNG (`diagnostics_{etf}_{side}.png`) with 15 subplots, saved to `day-model/plots/` and embedded above.
