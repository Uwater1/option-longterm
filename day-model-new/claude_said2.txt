## New ideas, same family as your winners

**A. Execution-timing perturbation (parameter plateau check)** (will do in the future, when I turn it into longer horizon)
Jitter entry/exit bar ±1-2 bars (10:00→9:55/10:05, 14:35→14:25/14:45). Require Sortino stays positive across jitter grid, not just point config. Recent work frames this as searching for a stable "parameter plateau" region rather than a single optimal point — overfit configs live on knife-edge, real signal lives on a plateau. Cheap: just re-run `simulate_returns` with shifted bar index, no new stats machinery.

**B. Sortino jackknife (reuse your B1 machinery, new target)** 
You already jackknife 7 chunks for **IC sign**. Do same chunks, but jackknife **Sortino value**: drop each chunk, recompute Sortino on remaining 6. Require median leave-one-out Sortino > 0 AND CV of the 7 leave-one-out Sortinos below cap. This is structurally identical code to what you already run (cheap, same infra) but targets the metric that actually correlates with your OOS Sharpe fails, not IC. Given jackknife-on-sign already proven and bootstrap-CI-on-sortino (#3) worked — this is the natural hybrid, should be near-free to test.

**C. tanh-ramp / z-threshold perturbation**
Same plateau logic as A, applied to position-sizing param (z>0.5 threshold) instead of timing. ±20% sweep, require Sortino sign-stable across sweep. Catches features whose apparent edge depends on the exact sizing curve you happened to tune globally — a form of implicit overfitting your gate can't see because sizing is shared across all candidates.

**D. Vol-scaled cost stress (refine your winner, #6)** (already done, 2.5 ~ 3 (20 ~ 24bp) good)
You flat-doubled cost 8→16bps and it worked well (3.3). Try cost scaling with realized vol instead of flat multiplier — `cost_t = base_cost * (1 + k * realized_vol_t / vol_median)`. Punishes signals that fire disproportionately during vol spikes (worse fills, more slippage) — exactly the regime your #7 regime-gate was trying and failing to catch via static IC buckets. Stress-based version might succeed where the shape-based version (#7) didn't.

**E. Combine your two winners into AND-ensemble, not two separate gates** (tell me if its already done)
Right now #6 and #3-lite presumably run as independent pass/fail. Consider requiring survival of *both simultaneously on the same resampled path* — e.g. bootstrap-resample the block series AND apply 2x cost on each resample, take CI lower bound. Interaction effect might catch marginal survivors that pass each test alone via different lucky subsets.
